
# Value Proposition Document

## Overview
**Delivery Pilot** is a self‑paced learning system on **deliverypilot.net** for professionals and prosumers who want to learn **end‑to‑end delivery in an AI‑ and agent‑driven world**.

Learners progress through a structured pathway:

1. **Assess** – Evaluate current skills  and understand their gaps
2. **Initial Setup** – Establish a baseline  using tools avaible to them
3. **Ongoing Work** – Continuous progress tracking  by getting simulation results and appending the scores.
4. **Simulations** – Guided, realistic practice scenarios  with video and inteactive form support.

Participants receive:
- A personalized **report** for the taken assesments.  
- An **invitation to simulations** based on progress
- Certifications when they are able to complete the delivery pilot program and build real world application that are signed in linkedin

---

## Simulations

Each simulation includes:

- **Top section:** Instructional video  
- **Bottom section:** Step‑by‑step instructions  
- Videos hosted as **unlisted YouTube links**, similar to *Barış Özcan’s style*  unlisted in the rifat erdem sahin youtube channel
- After completing tasks, learners **post their work to LinkedIn** tagging *Delivery Pilot* to receive community feedback  

**Self‑service simulations:** Free  
**Enterprise simulations:** Paid, with deeper interaction and guidance onsite and on teams

Simulations are built to:
- Teach step‑by‑step delivery  
- Enable feedback loops  
- Help users close execution gaps  
- Provide a safe space to learn by doing

The self learner get a certificate at the end of the journey which proves they can deliver solutions.
