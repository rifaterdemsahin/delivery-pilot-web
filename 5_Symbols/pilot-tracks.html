✈️ Pilot Tracks: FDE or Founder

AI is changing which jobs exist. The people who can take AI from demo to production are in demand. Delivery Pilot trains you to do that, and then you choose how to use it: get hired, or build your own business.

🛫 Every Pilot ships first

A Delivery Pilot is anyone who ships an AI system to production. A proof of concept doesn't count. It has to be live, used and secured.

Launch checklist (both tracks)
  • ✅ A live system that real users can access
  • ✅ A working Stripe checkout or a real internal user
  • 🔒 Security check: secrets kept in env or a vault, webhook signatures verified, nothing sensitive in Git
  • 🔧 A README that documents known technical debt
  • 📓 Weekly build logs shared with the community

🧭 Then choose your track

Everyone uses the same curriculum and starts with the same free project. Only the finish line differs, and you can switch tracks at any time.

🏢 FDE Track

For people who want to be employed. This track prepares you for forward deployed engineer roles, which are also advertised as applied AI engineer or AI solutions engineer. These engineers work inside companies to get AI working in production.

  • Write up your launch as a client engagement case study
  • Build a Git portfolio aimed at FDE job specs
  • Practise interviews with the community
🏁 Finish line: an FDE or applied-AI interview or offer, or an AI system shipped inside your current employer.

🚀 Founder Track

For people who want to be self-employed. Take a free, MIT-licensed starter business, make it your own, and sell it.

  • Claim a niche so that only one pilot runs each niche
  • Launch within 14 days of claiming it
  • Share your numbers and lessons openly
🏁 Finish line: your first paying customer.

🗺️ How it works

  1. Join the community. Pick your track and claim a project.
  2. Build. Start from the free starter business, which comes with the payments backend already built.
  3. Launch. Pass the launch checklist above to earn the 🛫 Launched badge.
  4. Reach your finish line. Earn the 🏢 FDE Pilot or 🚀 Founder Pilot badge.
  5. Get certified. Your shipped work counts as Git-based evidence towards full Pilot Certification.
  6. Pay it forward. Help a newer pilot reach launch.
