How the business model works

A business sponsors the students. Certified professionals close the skill gap and build the bots. Delivery Pilot runs those bots as a managed service.

1. Business sponsors the students

VIP is $10 a month. That sale funds the learners: self learners on Standard, which is free, and prosumers on Premium, which is $1 a month.

2. Professionals get certified and build the bots

They define a role, a domain, and the topics the bot owns, then build the bot. Premium includes full course access, active 1-1 sessions, and hands-on bot setup.

3. We support the business as a managed service

Managed bots are $100 per bot. We secure the bot, adapt it when the business changes, and train the people who use it. The business stays on the output: the role, the domain, and the topics.

Proof: the marketing bots

The working proof is the marketing bots we are already securing, adapting, and training.
