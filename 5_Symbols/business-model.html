The B2C2B loop

We turn AI learners into certified delivery pilots, and delivery pilots into enterprise results. A business sale sponsors the learners. Certified professionals close the skill gap and build the bots. Delivery Pilot runs those bots as a managed service — and the enterprise's staff come back into the loop as learners.

1. 🎓 B2C — professionals and students prepare for frontier-lab certifications

Professionals and students prepare for frontier-lab certifications with us. Students get free access. VIP is $10 a month and that sale funds the learners: self learners on Standard, which is free, and prosumers on Premium, which is $1 a month.

2. 📜 Certified — the lab certifies them, not us

They define a role, a domain, and the topics the bot owns, then build the bot. Premium includes full course access, active 1-1 sessions, and hands-on bot setup. The frontier lab issues the certification, so credibility is borrowed instead of asking people to trust a new badge.

3. 🚀 C2B — certified graduates become the delivery pilots

Our certified graduates build and run bots for enterprises under our managed service. Managed bots are $100 per bot. We secure the bot, adapt it when the business changes, and train the people who use it.

4. 🏢 B — enterprises get transformation, staff come back as learners

Enterprises get AI transformation without the jungle and stay on the output: the role, the domain, and the topics. Their staff come back into the loop as learners, and the project supplies a real case study for the next cohort.

Proof: the marketing bots

The working proof is the marketing bots we are already securing, adapting, and training.

🔁 Each stage feeds the next: learners supply talent, talent supplies delivery capacity, and enterprise projects supply real case studies for the next cohort.
