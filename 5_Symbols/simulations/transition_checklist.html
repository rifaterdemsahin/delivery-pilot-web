The Delivery Pilot Transition Checklist 🚀

Shift your mindset from specific, rigid coding languages to becoming an X-shaped professional who orchestrates AI to deliver end-to-end solutions.

Start Your Transition 🔄

Enter your email to track your transition into an X-shaped professional.

Pilot: (Change)
1. Foundation & Digital Presence 🏗️
Retire the Traditional Resume
Move away from static documents that get buried; your proof of work is now your primary currency.
Open/Audit your GitHub Account
If you don't have one, create it. This is your "business card" that shows your repo count, achievements, and commit frequency.
Adopt the "Wizard" Mindset
Stop identifying as just a coder or tester. Embrace a multi-disciplinary role that includes coding, testing, and operations.
2. The Assessment Phase (Diagnostics) 🩺
Visit DeliveryPilot.net
Register your email to enter the ecosystem.
Take the Skill Gap Assessment
Start the journey by entering your details to understand your current baseline.
Begin with "First Principles"
Answer the basic diagnostic questions (e.g., checking internet speed/latency) to identify what is "smoking or leaking" in your skill set.
3. Practical Simulation (The Build) 💻
Set Up Your Local Environment
Use git clone to replicate project repositories to your local compute.
Leverage AI Agents
Don't write every line of code manually. Use AI agents within your IDE to create tasks and generate scripts (e.g., PowerShell/PS1 scripts).
Iterative Implementation
Prompt the agent to create the solution (e.g., a connection checker).
Review and approve the agent's plan.
Execute and verify the code works in real-time.
Document as You Go
Ensure the agent updates the README and creates logs (like report.log) with timestamps for every run.
4. Shipping & Validation 🚢
Commit and Push
Finalize your project with an "Initial Commit" and host it on GitHub.
Submit for Feedback
Send your output back to the Delivery Pilot system to get your score and professional evaluation.
Repeat for Complexity
Move from simple scripts to complex, multi-layered solutions using the same "Assessment → Simulation → Shipping" protocol.
