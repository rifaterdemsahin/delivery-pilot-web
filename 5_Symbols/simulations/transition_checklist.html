The Delivery Pilot Transition Checklist 🚀

Shift your mindset from specific, rigid coding languages to becoming an X-shaped professional who orchestrates AI to deliver end-to-end solutions.

✅ Interaction Checklist

1. Foundation & Digital Presence 🏗️
Move away from static documents; your proof of work is your currency.
Your "business card" showing repo count and commit frequency.
Embrace a multi-disciplinary role asking "What" and "Why", not just "How".
2. The Assessment Phase 🩺
Enter the ecosystem to track progress.
Establish your current baseline.
Identify what is "smoking or leaking" (e.g. latency, tools).
3. Practical Simulation 💻
Clone project repositories to your local compute.
Use AI within your IDE to create tasks and generate scripts.
Prompt agent for solution
Review agent's plan
Execute and verify
Ensure README and logs are updated.
4. Shipping & Validation 🚢
Finalize "Initial Commit" and host on GitHub.

🐙 Repository Setup
