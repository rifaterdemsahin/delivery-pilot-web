The 3 Core Components of Delivery Pilot

A comprehensive program empowering professionals to deliver AI agent-based solutions effectively.

🔍

1. Skills Gap Assessments

Identifying Current Capabilities and Development Areas

Thorough assessments to identify professionals' current capabilities and pinpoint areas for development in AI agent implementation.

  • 🔒 Private & Secure: 1-1 sessions are strictly confidential.
  • 💻 Developers: Share your Git repositories (public or private).
  • ✍️ Non-Developers: Share your prompts and workflows.
  • 🎯 Identify Delivery Gaps: Pinpoint specific skill gaps and delivery bottlenecks in AI agent implementation.
  • 📄 Outcome: Everyone receives a unique maturity assessment report containing a tailored training roadmap for the AI courses.
🎓

2. Collaborative Simulation Workshops

Hands-on Learning to Build AI Agent Solutions

Through hands-on simulation workshops, participants learn to build solutions with AI agents collaboratively, gaining practical experience in real-world scenarios. This is a 10-step process to reach a secure, explainable AI product for the enterprise as a team delivery.

⚠️ Note: One-to-many format can include multiple consultants for bigger groups.

📚 Curriculum Includes:
  • ✨ Vibe Programming
  • 🤖 RAF & Xazi Frameworks
  • 🧠 Model Training & Security
  • 🔄 Hybrid AI Modeling

3. Pilot Certification

Certifying Qualified Pilots for AI Agent Delivery

The program certifies professionals as qualified pilots for delivering AI agent applications, with specific focus on critical metrics.

  • 🔧 Technical Debt Management: Learn best practices for managing and reducing technical debt in AI agent implementations.
  • 🔒 Security Compliance: Master security best practices and compliance requirements for enterprise AI agent applications.
  • 💻 Git-Based Product Outputs: Real deliverables demonstrating practical skills and closing execution gaps.
  • 🏆 Certification Achievement: Become a certified pilot qualified to deliver AI agent applications in enterprise environments.
