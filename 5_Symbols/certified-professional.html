How to Add Your Project to LinkedIn

Follow these steps to showcase your verified Delivery Pilot project on your LinkedIn profile.

Step 1: Add Project

Go to your LinkedIn profile and find the "Suggested for you" section or the "Projects" section. Click on "Add project" to begin showcasing your skills through real-world work.

Step 2: Define Project

Enter the title of your GitHub delivery project. Use a clear, descriptive name that reflects the AI transformation you completed (e.g., "Enterprise AI Documentation Agent Implementation").

Step 3: Associate & Details

Associate the project with your current role or "Delivery Pilot". Add the GitHub repository URL and a detailed description of your contributions as a delivery professional.

Step 4: Publish

Review your project details and click "Save". Your project will now be visible on your profile, certifying your ability to deliver completed AI transformations.
