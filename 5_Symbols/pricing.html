How the Business Model Works

Delivery Pilot is a business-to-consumer-to-business practice. A business sponsors the people who need the skill. Those people get certified, build the bots, and we run the bots for the business as a managed service.

1 Business sponsors the students

VIP membership is how a business pays in. That sale funds the learners — self learners on Standard, prosumers on Premium — who close the skill gap the business actually has.

↓
2 Professionals get certified and build the bots

The people closing the skill gap are certified as Delivery Pilots. They define a role, a domain, and the topics the bot is allowed to own, then build the bot for that future job.

↓
3 We support the business as a managed service

Delivery Pilot then runs those bots as a managed service provider at $100 per bot. The Delivery Pilot template keeps the software development life cycle going: secure the bot, adapt it when the business changes, and train the people who use it. The business stays on the output — the role, the domain, and the topics — and the delivery risk of the AI transformation stays with the practice.

Proof: the marketing bots

The proof is already in production as marketing bots. We are securing them so they stay inside a defined role and domain, adapting them as the message and the audience change, and training the cohort that will run the next one. See the examples on the sample bots page. The catalog lives at github.com/rifaterdemsahin/bots.
