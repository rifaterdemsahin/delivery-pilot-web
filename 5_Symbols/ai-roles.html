🧭 AI Roles You Can Do

AI is changing which jobs exist. These are roles where people who can ship AI are in demand. Each one maps to a Delivery Pilot track and a first project you can start this week.

Job titles vary between companies. FDE roles are also advertised as applied AI engineer or AI solutions engineer.

🏢

Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)

🏢 FDE track

What you do: Works inside a client company to get AI running in production: connects models to real data, workflows and systems, then proves the value.

Key skills: APIs and integrations, agents and tool use, security, talking to stakeholders

Your first project: Ship the starter business and write it up as a client engagement case study.

🧪

Applied AI Engineer

🏢 FDE track

What you do: Builds AI features into products: retrieval (RAG), prompts, evaluations and guardrails, so the feature works reliably for real users.

Key skills: Python or TypeScript, LLM APIs, RAG and vector search, evals

Your first project: Add a RAG feature to your project and measure its answers with a small eval set.

🧩

AI Solutions Engineer / Architect

🏢 FDE track

What you do: Designs the AI solution before it is built: picks models, patterns and platforms, and explains the trade-offs to customers and teams.

Key skills: System design, cloud, agent design patterns, cost and risk trade-offs

Your first project: Draw the architecture of your shipped project and explain one decision you would change.

🤖

AI Agent Engineer

🏢 FDE or 🚀 Founder

What you do: Builds agents that take actions with tools: they plan, call APIs, recover from errors and hand off to humans when needed.

Key skills: Tool use, MCP, orchestration, observability, safe permissions

Your first project: Build one agent that does a real task end to end and logs every step.

⚙️

AI Automation Specialist

🚀 Founder track

What you do: Automates business workflows with AI and tools like n8n: inboxes, documents, reports and hand-offs between systems.

Key skills: Workflow tools, APIs and webhooks, prompt design, process mapping

Your first project: Automate one painful workflow for a real person and measure the hours saved.

🔁

LLMOps / MLOps Engineer

🏢 FDE track

What you do: Keeps AI systems running in production: deployment, monitoring, cost control, model updates and rollbacks.

Key skills: CI/CD, containers and Kubernetes, monitoring, cost tracking

Your first project: Add monitoring and a cost dashboard to your shipped project.

🔒

AI Security Engineer

🏢 FDE track

What you do: Protects AI systems from prompt injection, data leaks and unsafe tool use, and checks they meet security rules.

Key skills: Threat modelling, secrets management, red-teaming, access control

Your first project: Red-team your own project: try to break it, then document and fix what you found.

📊

AI Evaluator / Quality Engineer

🏢 FDE track

What you do: Measures whether AI output is good enough: builds test sets, scores answers and catches regressions before users do.

Key skills: Test design, domain knowledge, data analysis, attention to detail

Your first project: Write 30 test questions for an AI feature and track its score over a week.

🧭

AI Product Manager

🏢 FDE or 🚀 Founder

What you do: Decides what AI should build and why: finds the problem worth solving, sets success measures and works with engineers to ship.

Key skills: User research, specs, metrics, knowing what AI can and cannot do

Your first project: Write a one-page spec for an AI feature, ship it and report what users did.

🛡️

AI Governance & Compliance Lead

🏢 FDE track

What you do: Makes sure AI is used responsibly and legally: policies, risk reviews, data protection and audit trails.

Key skills: Regulation (for example GDPR and the EU AI Act), risk assessment, policy writing

Your first project: Write a short AI use policy and risk review for your own project.

📈

AI Transformation & Enablement Coach

🚀 Founder track

What you do: Helps teams change how they work with AI: training, new workflows and showing where AI creates value.

Key skills: Coaching, change management, value recognition, hands-on AI tools

Your first project: Run one workshop where a team ships a small AI workflow and measures the result.

🎓

AI Educator & Content Creator

🚀 Founder track

What you do: Teaches AI to others through courses, videos and communities, turning hands-on experience into lessons people can follow.

Key skills: Clear explaining, video and writing, real projects to show

Your first project: Publish a breakdown of something you shipped, using the reverse-engineer method.

💼

AI Implementation Consultant

🚀 Founder track

What you do: Sells and delivers AI projects to businesses as an independent: scopes the work, builds it and hands it over.

Key skills: Scoping and pricing, delivery, client communication, a portfolio of shipped work

Your first project: Land your first paying client and deliver one small project end to end.
