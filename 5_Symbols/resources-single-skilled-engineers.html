A Day in the Life

The Impossible Day

Meet Sarah, a single-skilled backend engineer trying to navigate a typical day in the new world:

  • 9:00 AM: Three production alerts arrive simultaneously—database performance, API errors, and cache invalidation
  • 9:45 AM: While investigating, asked to review 4 urgent pull requests
  • 10:30 AM: Security team reports 8 critical vulnerabilities needing immediate patching
  • 11:00 AM: Deployment pipeline fails across 3 environments, blocking 6 teams
  • 1:00 PM: Customer escalation requires diving into unfamiliar service code
  • 2:30 PM: Infrastructure team needs input on scaling issues affecting 5 services
  • 4:00 PM: Incident postmortem meeting for yesterday's outage, while new alerts keep arriving

By 6:00 PM, Sarah has addressed only 30% of the issues, made mistakes due to context-switching, and has 20+ more items added to her backlog. There's simply no way to combat this effectively alone.
