The Documentation Decay Problem

In modern enterprise environments, technology upgrades are constant. Human-written documentation is obsolete the moment it is published.

🐌

The Speed Constraint

Operators must upgrade systems frequently for security and performance. Manual documentation cannot keep pace with 10+ deployments per day.

📉

Information Asymmetry

The "Live State" of the system and the "Documented State" diverge rapidly, leading to failed SRE investigations and dangerous operational assumptions.

😫

Documentation Toil

Engineers spend up to 30% of their time updating wikis, diagrams, and READMEs instead of solving core architectural problems.

Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Orchestration

We don't remove the human; we elevate them. In our framework, agents do the Action (scanning code, writing markdown, generating Mermaid diagrams) while the human provides the Intent and Final Validation.

This ensures that documentation is not just "AI-generated noise" but Verified System Intelligence.
